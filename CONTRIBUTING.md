# Contributing

Participation is voluntary and unpaid. Explicitly opt in on an issue; proposing work does not make you a team member. Agree a bounded deliverable and acceptance criteria with the maintainer before starting. No promises of payment or revenue share.

1. Read the design, roadmap, asset policy, and open issues. Choose one task or propose a small one.
2. Comment with the intended change, evidence or relevant experience, and available time. Maintainer records agreed scope and dependencies. Do not assign others without consent.
3. Fork the repository; create a descriptive branch. Install Git LFS before adding binary content. Coordinate ownership of maps/assets; use LFS locks when supported and available to your account, otherwise reserve the asset in the issue.
4. Submit a focused pull request linking the issue, describing behavior and test results, including limitations. Provide before/after video for movement or visual changes and network conditions for replication changes.
5. Maintainer reviews scope, provenance, acceptance criteria, and regressions before merge. No automatic execution of untrusted contributor scripts or access to operator secrets.

Original code/docs use MIT. Submit only material you have authority to contribute under the stated license. Every asset needs a provenance entry (path, creator, source, license, modifications, attribution). Engine source and licensed marketplace content must not be committed to this public repo. Keep external dependencies minimal and review licenses before adding them.

Treat other contributors respectfully. Keep feedback specific and reproducible. Report bugs with commit, engine version, platform, steps, expected/actual result, server/client logs, and network conditions; redact personal data and credentials.
