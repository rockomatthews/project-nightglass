# Confirmed product direction: partnered crypto matches

Operator confirmed 2026-10-16: two teams of two players, best of three rounds, wagering against each other; the winning team takes the agreed pot. Chappie's proposed recruiting internship is voluntary and unpaid.

This is a future product goal. M0 and the first playable use no-value practice matches. No deposits, wallets, tokens, settlement service or live wagers exist in this project.

## Match design questions before wagering implementation

First to two round wins ends the series, at 2–0 or 2–1. Because attackers and defenders have different capabilities, merely alternating sides gives one team the same role twice. Test and decide a fair decider/side-selection rule before this format can support stakes. Do not silently convert the user-requested rounds into paired maps. Keep team identity stable across role swaps.

Stake asset/chain, contribution per player, teammate payout split, fees, cancellation/no-show rules, disconnect treatment, disputes, match result authority and settlement finality remain undecided. No chain or token is presumed from other HOODED projects. A game-server result alone is not an independently verified basis for releasing funds.

## Delivery gates

1. Reliable no-value best-of-three gameplay, measured side balance, repeatable results and tamper-resistant server events.
2. Written economic and threat model: agreed stakes/payouts, participant consent, funded escrow model, abuse/collusion and result-dispute handling, refunds and operational failure behavior.
3. Applicable age/location eligibility and legal requirements assessed for intended launch markets before accepting stakes; no assumption that crypto or skill competition removes those requirements.
4. Separately reviewed sandbox/testnet integration and adversarial tests, with no real funds.
5. Separate operator approval and completed security/operational review before any live-money launch.

Recruit contributors for game development now. Do not promise earnings, launch dates, compensation or access to wallets. Chappie has no spending, hiring-budget or settlement authority.
