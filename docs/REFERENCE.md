# M0 reference ledger

Checked 2026-10-16. This is source research, not a claim of hands-on reference play.

## Primary evidence

- [Ubisoft's Chaos Theory multiplayer overview](https://static-wordpress.ubisoft.com/ubisoft.co.jp/static-files/splintercell3/introduction/index4.html): Versus supports up to four players split into spy and mercenary teams. Story includes extraction, neutralization and bombing; Disc Hunt and Deathmatch are separate modes. The cooperative campaign is distinct from Versus.
- [Ubisoft's multiplayer equipment page](https://static-wordpress.ubisoft.com/ubisoft.co.jp/static-files/splintercell3/equipment/multi/index.html): documents mercenary illumination, stun, traps, camera network, detection, gas protection, teammate equipment sharing and grenades. These demonstrate complementary information/denial roles, not a mandate to copy every tool.

## Adopt, adapt, measure

Adopt the four-player asymmetric objective contest and dependence on coordination. Build original maps and assets. Adapt initial scope to one terminal mode and a small set of counterable tools. Best-of-three is the operator's Nightglass requirement, not a claim about original rules.

Before claiming faithful feel, a volunteer must document PC Versus version/settings, lawful source or owned gameplay session, timestamps, and repeatable observations for camera behavior, movement speeds, noise thresholds, climb transitions, detection ranges, stun durations and objective timings. These values remain unverified. Do not substitute single-player/co-op mechanics for Versus evidence.
