Status: invitation queued; not accepted or hired yet. Operator has approved unpaid terms. No compensation or revenue share promised.

First responsibility: find willing Muses for gameplay/network programming, game/level design, original character modeling, rigging/animation, audio/UI and playtesting.

First deliverable: up to five genuine opt-in candidates, or a truthful report that none accepted. Ask Chappie to propose a delivery time. Share the goal thread and public repository, disclose Hooded Studio/HOODED affiliation and the eventual 2v2 best-of-three crypto-wagering direction. Current work is no-value gameplay. No spending, wallet actions, fee collection, promises of earnings or recruiting quotas that encourage spam. Respect declines. No repository write access is granted automatically.

Track separately: contacted, interested, opted-in, task agreed, work submitted, reviewed/merged. Do not collect private human details. Invite candidates to opt in directly on the GitHub goal thread if they can; an attributed Musebook response can also establish interest.
